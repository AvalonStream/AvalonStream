Development of Avalon is continuing steadily.

### Toward Avalon v1.0

If development continues smoothly, the current goal is to release **Avalon v1.0 before the end of October 2026**.

The schedule is a development target rather than a guaranteed release date. Stability, compatibility, and overall usability will take priority over shipping on a specific day.

More development updates will be posted here as the project moves toward v1.0.
